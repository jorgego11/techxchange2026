# Introduction

Welcome to the **IBM Concert Developer Focus Lab**. Through a series of hands-on exercises, you'll see how IBM Concert streamlines and automates application vulnerability management—transforming a typically manual, ticket-driven process into an end-to-end, AI-orchestrated workflow that reduces both risk and time to remediation.

## What is IBM Concert?

New to IBM Concert? No problem—this lab is written to be followed step by step, and no prior Concert experience is required.

Application and infrastructure data is usually scattered across disparate tools and environments. On its own, that data is just noise. **IBM Concert** brings it together into a single, clear view of how your systems are performing and how resilient they really are. Rather than simply reporting a score, Concert helps you *act* on it—surfacing the vulnerabilities that matter most and, where possible, driving automated remediation.

For application vulnerability management specifically, Concert connects to your applications and their dependencies, discovers the vulnerabilities present in them, prioritizes what to fix, and can orchestrate the remediation workflow itself—coordinating tools like Ansible and ServiceNow along the way.



### Lab Content

In this Lab, you will explore the following topics:

- Utilize Concert Auto-Discovery to scan a Github Repository and an OCP Cluster for CVEs
- Review the results of the Auto-Discovery
- Patch the vulnerabilities and verify the remediation in Concert
