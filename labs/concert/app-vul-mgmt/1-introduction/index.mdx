# Introduction

Welcome to the **IBM Concert Application Vulnerability Management Lab**. Through a series of hands-on exercises you'll see how IBM Concert streamlines and automates application vulnerability management — turning a typically manual, ticket-driven process into an end-to-end, AI-orchestrated workflow that reduces both risk and time to remediation.

## What is IBM Concert?

No prior Concert experience is required. This lab is designed to be followed step by step, and everything you need is explained along the way.

Application and infrastructure data usually lives scattered across disparate tools and environments, and on its own that data is just noise. **IBM Concert** brings it all together into a single, clear view of how your systems are performing and how resilient they really are. Rather than just reporting a score, Concert helps you act on it — surfacing the vulnerabilities that matter most and, where possible, driving automated remediation.

For application vulnerability management specifically, Concert:

- Connects to your applications and their dependencies
- Discovers the vulnerabilities present across both source code and running container images
- Prioritizes what to fix based on real risk in *your* environment
- Orchestrates the remediation workflow, coordinating tools such as IBM Bob, Ansible, and ServiceNow

## Key Concepts

Before you begin, it helps to know a few terms used throughout the lab:

- **CVE** — Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures. A publicly disclosed security flaw assigned a unique identifier such as `CVE-2021-44228`.
- **CVSS** — Common Vulnerability Scoring System. An industry-standard severity rating from 0 to 10 that measures how dangerous a flaw is *in general*.
- **EPSS** — Exploit Prediction Scoring System. An AI model that predicts the probability a CVE will be exploited within the next 30 days.
- **NVD** - National Vulnerability Database. U.S. government repository of standards-based vulnerability management data, maintained by the National Institute 
of Standards and Technology (NIST).
- **Blast radius** — Concert's dependency-impact graph showing which assets are affected by a specific vulnerability and by what path.
- **Risk score** — Concert's own score (1–10) combining CVSS severity, EPSS exploitability, and the business context you provide to measure how much a 
vulnerability matters *in your environment*.
