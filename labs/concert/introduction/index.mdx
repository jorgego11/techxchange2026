- [**Lab 2614-IBM Concert Protect: Automating WebSphere Liberty Patching & Vulnerability Remediation**](../websphere-vuln/1-introduction/index.mdx)
  - In this Lab, we explore how Concert can auto remediate WebSphere ND and Liberty vulnerabilities.
